What counts as an error (and how you handle it)
– Technical aborts (fatal) → stop & log
– Invalid rows → skip & log
– DB transaction errors → rollback
– HTTP/REST errors → no file created
– Validation/conversion errors → row is excluded

Not a technical error, but needs business rules

– Price changes that are not wanted
– Stock = 0 hides products (may or may not be desired)
